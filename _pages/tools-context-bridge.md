## What is Context Bridge?

A browser extension that lets you **save and transfer your AI chat conversations** between different platforms. Your data stays private with local encryption - nothing is stored online.

Whether you're switching from ChatGPT to Claude, or want to continue a conversation on a different device, Context Bridge makes it seamless.

## Privacy First

Context Bridge is built with privacy as a core principle:

- **No external servers** - Your conversations never leave your device
- **Local encryption** - AES-256-GCM with 100,000 PBKDF2 iterations
- **No tracking** - No analytics, no data collection
- **Open about privacy** - [Read our Privacy Policy](/context-bridge/privacy-policy.html)

---

**Tech:** TypeScript, Chrome Extension APIs (Manifest V3), Web Crypto API, AES-256-GCM
